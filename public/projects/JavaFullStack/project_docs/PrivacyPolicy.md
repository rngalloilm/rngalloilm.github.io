## Policy
Your given information. We will store your name, username, email, and a hash of your password. We will never store your password in plaintext. For staff members, we will store the location at which you work.
Information we collect. We store a log of all user orders. Any time you make a purchase using our application, we will store both the purchase, and which user the purchase is associated with.

## Choice
Anonymity. We allow for guest orders, which will only store that the order happened, and whether it was picked up or not. You do not always need to log in to use our service.

## Access
Order History. You can view your personal orders whenever you like at your "Orders" page. You may also request to change what information we store about you in our system. If you wish us to not store your name, email, etc., you may request this.

## Security
Passwords. Your passwords are stored as hashed values. We will never store your passwords as plaintext. All requests made to the server with sensitive content will be encrypted while in motion.
Personal Data. All of your personal data is stored using a MySQL database, which cannot be accessed remotely. This means your data cannot be compromised by an outside actor without direct access to one of our team’s laptops. The team has gone through a careful vetting process to ensure only the best and most trustworthy members have access to your data.
Threat Defense. We sanitize all inputs to both our forms and requests to our server to ensure that there are no data leakages through methods such as prompt injection. This ensures that the only way to get your data is by physically stealing one of our hard drives.

## Redress
Error Support. If you believe that we are not doing a good enough job in terms of security, think we should change our policies, or would like to choose to not share a particular piece of data, please email our support correspondent at aaparr@ncsu.edu.
Failure of Standards. If we have not managed to uphold any of the agreements as listed above, you are entitled to legal action. Particularly in terms of data, the GDPR must be enforced if you are a member of the EU.

## Updates
Notification. We will notify all of our users via email whenever we make a substantial change to our policy. This includes anything like changing the base policy or possibly even removing certain aspects.
Constant visibility. This form will always be visible to users, so everyone will be able to access this policy and view any changes made so they can act based on the most recent version.
